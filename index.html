Anti-raid protection

Raids end here.

Spectre is a free Discord bot that spots a flood of new accounts, locks your server down and pings your mods, before anyone panics. It also covers anti-nuke, moderation, tickets and verification.

Join the DiscordSee the commands

What it does

  • Stops raids and nukes. If a pile of accounts join at once, Shield Mode turns on. Your moderators get pinged and the owner gets a DM. /lockdown locks every channel when you need it.
  • Moderates. Warn, mute, kick, ban, tempban, purge, lock and slowmode, with notes and a case history. People are told by DM when action is taken.
  • Escalates warnings. Set your own ladder, like three warnings for a mute and eight for a ban. Warnings expire.
  • Filters the junk. Spam, caps, mass mentions, invites, links, profanity, and fake-Nitro style scam links, including edited messages.
  • Checks newcomers. Button or code verification, several roles on success, and an unverified role that comes off when they pass.
  • Handles tickets and appeals. Private tickets with transcripts, and banned or muted members can appeal from the DM.
  • Welcomes people. A greeting you write, an automatic role, and button panels so members pick their own roles.
  • Keeps logs. Cases, edits and deletions, joins, role and channel changes, in the channels you choose.

Spectre can also speak in three styles, set with /style: embed for boxed cards, inline for light formatted text, and text for plain text. It applies to replies, logs, alerts and DMs.

Commands

Everything is a slash command. /help in your server shows them too.

Setting it up

  1. Add Spectre to your server (join the Discord for the invite and help) and keep its role above the roles it should manage.
  2. Run /config setup to pick a log channel and your moderator role.
  3. Run /verify setup if you want newcomers gated until they pass.
  4. Run /shield status to see the raid settings and set where alerts go.

Cost

Nothing. There are no tiers or locked features. If it's saved you some trouble and you'd like to help with hosting, there's a tip link. It's optional and unlocks nothing.

What it keeps

Stored: your server's settings; cases (warns, mutes, kicks, bans, notes) with user ID, moderator ID, reason, time and appeal text; ticket status; verification join time while someone is unverified; and temp-ban timers.

Not stored: message content, files, voice, or DMs. Edit and delete logs go to your own log channel.

Deleting it: /data info shows what's kept, /data delete_user erases one person's records, and /data delete_server erases everything for your server. If Spectre leaves a server, its data is erased after 30 days.

Questions

What permissions does it need?

View channels, send messages, embed links, attach files, read message history, manage messages, channels and roles, manage server, kick, ban and timeout members, and view the audit log.

Why can't it give a role?

Discord only lets a bot manage roles below its own. Drag Spectre's role above the ones it should hand out.

Can I turn parts off?

/modules switches whole areas on or off, like tickets, verification or welcome messages. Your settings stay saved while a module is off.

Will it ping everyone in a raid?

No. Raid alerts ping only your moderator role, and the server owner gets a DM.
