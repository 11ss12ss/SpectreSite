Terms of Use

Last updated November 21, 2026

Spectre is a free Discord bot. These are the plain rules for using it. By adding Spectre to a server or using its dashboard, you agree to them.

1Using Spectre

Spectre is provided free and as-is, with no promise that it will always be online, bug-free or available. You must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines while you use it. You're responsible for how you configure Spectre in your server, and for what your moderators do with it.

2What gets Spectre removed

We can remove Spectre from any server that breaks one of these rules, and we can stop it being added again. This is the list we pick from when it happens, and the server owner is told which one applied.

2.1Breaking Discord's rules

Servers that break Discord's Terms of Service or Community Guidelines. If Discord wouldn't allow it, we won't help run it.

2.2Harassing, targeting or doxing people

Using Spectre's moderation tools, logs or data to harass, stalk, threaten or expose people, or to build records on someone to hurt them.

2.3Raids, spam and scams

Servers set up to raid other servers, mass-spam, run scams or phishing, spread malware, or evade bans at scale.

2.4Illegal or seriously harmful content

Servers built around illegal activity, or content that seriously harms people, including anything that sexualizes minors. This one is removed immediately and may be reported.

2.5Abusing Spectre

Exploiting bugs, attacking or overloading the bot or its dashboard, scraping it, trying to get at other servers' data, or trying to get around its limits or protections.

2.6Impersonating Spectre or its team

Pretending to be Spectre, its owner or its staff, or running a fake copy of the bot or dashboard to mislead people.

2.7Other breaches of these Terms

Anything else that breaks these Terms. When we use this one we add a short note saying what happened.

3If Spectre is removed

The server owner gets a message (when their DMs allow it) showing the server, the rule and a link to its section here. If you think it was a mistake, join the support server and tell us. We'll look at it and may let you add Spectre back. When Spectre leaves a server, that server's saved data is erased after 30 days.

4The dashboard

The dashboard lets you sign in with Discord and manage servers where you have the Manage Server permission. Keep your account secure. Don't share your session or try to access servers you don't manage. See the Privacy page for what it handles.

5Liability

Spectre is a free tool. To the extent the law allows, it comes with no warranties, and we aren't liable for losses from using it, from it being offline, or from it acting on your settings. Anti-raid tools reduce risk; they can't guarantee a server is safe.

We may update these Terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use Spectre after a change means you accept it.

7Contact

Questions or appeals: the support server.
