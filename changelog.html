What's new

Spectre is free, and it keeps getting better. Here is what changed and when.

v1.0.1 UpdateNovember 2026

  • Website support tickets. Open a ticket at spectrebot.org/support.html and follow it with a private ticket number and code.
  • Faster handling of urgent reports. Raid reports and server issues are flagged to the team straight away.
  • Add Spectre from the site. The "Add to Discord" button now works.
  • Safer staff area. The developer console sits behind a two-room login.
  • Live status page at spectrebot.org/status.html.

v1.0.0 Launch

  • Moderation, anti-raid with shield mode and anti-nuke, auto-moderation, tickets, verification, appeals, welcome messages and role panels.
  • A web dashboard to manage your server, and feedback forms on the site.
